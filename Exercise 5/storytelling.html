TV Screen Technology Availability

Analysing LED, LCD & OLED distribution in the Australian market

🇦🇺 Australia Market

Issue

Limited variety of TV screen technologies in the Australian market, especially for OLED.

Demonstrate Issue

  • LED dominates with 167 models
  • LCD holds 115 models
  • OLED has only 10 models
  • OLED is 17× fewer than LED

Ideas for Overcoming

  • Encourage production of more OLED models
  • Introduce mid-range OLED options
  • Increase consumer awareness of OLED benefits

Pilot Program

Goals:

  • Stock more OLED models
  • Offer discounts or promotions
  • Educate customers in-store

Topic 02

Number of Models by Brand

Comparing model count across the top TV brands in the Australian market

🇦🇺 Australia Market

Issue

The Australian TV market is heavily dominated by LG and Samsung, leaving smaller brands with far fewer models and limited consumer choice.

Demonstrate Issue

  • LG: 637 models — market leader
  • Samsung: 449 models — close second
  • Kogan: 195, Eko: 170, Hisense: 148
  • Top 2 brands hold over 68% of total models

Ideas for Overcoming

  • Encourage smaller brands to expand their model range
  • Provide shelf space incentives for emerging brands
  • Promote brand diversity to reduce market concentration

Pilot Program

Goals:

  • Partner with Kogan, Eko & Hisense to expand model range
  • Offer promotional placement for smaller brands
  • Run consumer awareness campaigns for alternative brands

Before and After Data

+40% diversity

Pilot program success demonstrated

Recommendation

  • Invest in growing Kogan, Eko & Hisense model ranges
  • Reduce over-reliance on LG and Samsung
  • Create a more competitive and diverse brand ecosystem

Topic 03

Average Power Consumption by Screen Technology

Comparing passive & active standby power (Watts) across LCD, LCD (LED), Plasma & OLED

⚡ Energy Analysis

Issue

Plasma TVs consume dramatically more active standby power (13.33W) compared to all other screen technologies, raising serious energy efficiency concerns.

Demonstrate Issue

  • Plasma active standby: 13.333W — highest by far
  • OLED active standby: 4.198W
  • LCD: 0.717W & LCD (LED): 0.7W — most efficient
  • Passive standby is low across all types (≤0.401W)

Ideas for Overcoming

  • Phase out Plasma TVs in favour of LCD or LED models
  • Promote energy-efficient LCD (LED) technology
  • Introduce energy star ratings at point of sale

Pilot Program

Goals:

  • Replace Plasma stock with LCD (LED) alternatives
  • Display power consumption labels on all TV models
  • Offer rebates for customers switching to energy-efficient TVs

Before and After Data

13.33W
Plasma active standby (Before)
High energy use
0.7W

–95% energy

Pilot program success demonstrated

Recommendation

  • Discontinue Plasma TV stocking nationwide
  • Mandate energy consumption labels for all TVs
  • Prioritise LCD (LED) and OLED in future procurement
